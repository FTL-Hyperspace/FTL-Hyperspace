1. Put your mods in the `mods` folder inside your ftlman folder. The download already
   contains one.
2. Press **Scan** in the top right so ftlman picks up what you just added.
3. Tick the mods you want. Order matters here: drag them into place, because some mods
   have to sit above others. The mod's own page will say when it does.
4. Press **Apply**.
