
Deleting the mod files is not enough on its own. `ftl.dat` still holds the patched
content and has to be put back to vanilla.

<Tabs syncKey="manager">
<TabItem label="ftlman">

Delete `ftl.dat` and rename `ftl.dat.vanilla` back to `ftl.dat`.

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Slipstream">

Slipstream keeps no vanilla copy. Untick every mod and press **Patch** again: patching
with nothing selected is what writes the original `ftl.dat` back.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>
