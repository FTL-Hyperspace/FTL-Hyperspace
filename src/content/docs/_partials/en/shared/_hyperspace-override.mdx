<Aside type="caution" title="Keep Hyperspace.ftl on top">
  `Hyperspace.ftl` must sit above every other mod in the list. Mods that ship
  their own `hyperspace.xml`, Multiverse among them, are patched after it and
  override it. Patching hyperspace.ftl below other mods may lead to a broken
  install.
</Aside>
