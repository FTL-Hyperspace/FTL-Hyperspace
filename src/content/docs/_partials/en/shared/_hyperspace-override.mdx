<Aside type="caution" title="Some mods override Hyperspace.ftl">
Some mods don't require `Hyperspace.ftl` as they contain their own `Hyperspace.xml`, 
Multiverse is among them. If you are using one of those, leave `Hyperspace.ftl` 
**unticked** in the mod list. Patching both will cause a broken install.
</Aside>
