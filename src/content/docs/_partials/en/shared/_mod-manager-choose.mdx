You need a mod manager to patch `.ftl` files into the game. **ftlman is the one to
use** unless a mod misbehaves under it. The rest of this guide follows whichever tab
you pick.

<Aside type="tip" title="Moving over from Slipstream">

first run and your mod order carries across automatically.
