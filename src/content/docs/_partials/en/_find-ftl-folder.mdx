On Steam, right-click **FTL: Faster Than Light** in your library and choose
**Manage → Browse local files**. Steam opens the folder for you, which is safer than
typing the path by hand.

<Screenshot src={browseLocalFiles} alt="Right-clicking FTL in the Steam library and choosing Manage then Browse local files" label="Show me where that is in Steam" />

Got it from a different store? The folder is wherever you installed it.
