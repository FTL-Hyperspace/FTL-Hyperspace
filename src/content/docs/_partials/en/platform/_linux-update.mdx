<TabItem label="Manual">

<Steps>

1. Download `FTL.Hyperspace.x.xx.x-Linux.zip` from the [latest release](link:releases).

2. Replace the `Hyperspace.*.so` file in your FTL folder with the one for your FTL
   version: `Hyperspace.1.6.13.amd64.so` or `Hyperspace.1.6.12.amd64.so`.

   <VersionCheck />

3. Copy `Hyperspace.ftl` from the ZIP into your mod manager's `mods` folder, replacing
   the old one.

4. Run your mod manager again and patch your mods.

</Steps>
