<FtlmanIntro />

<Steps>

1. ### Steam settings

   Skip this if you did not buy FTL on Steam.

   <SteamSettings />

   <WindowsSaves />

   <BackupSaves />

2. ### Roll FTL back to 1.6.9

   <Rollback />

3. ### Set up ftlman

   <Ftlman />

4. ### Install Hyperspace

   <FtlmanHyperspace />

5. ### Add your mods

   <FtlmanMods />

   <Aside type="caution" title="Installing Multiverse?">
   Leave your mods out for now and follow the Multiverse section below instead.
   </Aside>

6. ### Check it worked

   <Verify />

   <Aside type="danger" title="Error mentioning AcheivementTracker">
   FTL did not downgrade to 1.6.9 successfully. Run the rollback again and confirm
   `FTLGame_orig.exe` was created.
   </Aside>

</Steps>

**Hyperspace is now installed and ready to play.** Everything below is optional.

## Adding Multiverse

<Multiverse ftlmanOnly />

## Updating

Open ftlman, pick the newer release from the **Hyperspace** dropdown and press
**Apply**. Your saves are not affected.

## Uninstalling

<FtlmanUninstall />

To go back to FTL 1.6.14 as well, delete `FTLGame.exe` and rename `FTLGame_orig.exe`
back to `FTLGame.exe`.
