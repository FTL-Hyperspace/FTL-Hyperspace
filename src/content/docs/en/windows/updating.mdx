<TabItem label="Manual">

<Steps>

1. Download `FTL.Hyperspace.x.xx.x-Windows.zip` from the [latest release](link:releases).

2. Replace `Hyperspace.dll` in your FTL folder, next to `FTLGame.exe`, with the one
   from the ZIP.

   <Aside type="tip" title="Check you are still on 1.6.9">
   Launch FTL and read the version from the **bottom right** of the main menu. Anything
   higher means Steam updated the game and put back its own `FTLGame.exe`, so
   [roll it back](../manual-install/#roll-ftl-back-to-169) again.
   </Aside>

3. Copy `Hyperspace.ftl` from the ZIP into your mod manager's `mods` folder, replacing
   the old one.

4. Run your mod manager again and patch your mods.

</Steps>
