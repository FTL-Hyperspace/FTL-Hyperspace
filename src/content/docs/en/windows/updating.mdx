Updating is quick, and your saves are not affected.

1. Download `FTL.Hyperspace.x.xx.x-Windows.zip` from the [latest release](link:releases).
2. Replace `Hyperspace.dll` in your FTL folder with the new one, along with any other
   DLLs in the ZIP.

You do not need to roll FTL back again. 1.6.9 stays 1.6.9.

<FtlmanUpdate />

<Aside type="tip" title="Check you are still on 1.6.9">
Launch FTL and read the version from the **bottom right** of the main menu. Anything
higher means Steam updated the game and put back its own `FTLGame.exe`, so
[roll it back](../manual-install/) before replacing the DLL.
</Aside>

<Aside type="caution" title="Running Multiverse or other mods?">
After updating hyperspace, check for the newest versions of the mods,
download them and then run your mod manager again and re-patch Multiverse.
</Aside>

<Aside type="note" title="Running other Hyperspace mods?">
If a mod ships its own `Hyperspace.ftl`, re-patch that too. Multiverse is the
exception, it should never have `Hyperspace.ftl` patched alongside it.
</Aside>
