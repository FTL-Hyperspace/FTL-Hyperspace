Back to desktop mode for this. Your saves are not affected.

1. Download `FTL.Hyperspace.x.xx.x-Linux.zip` from the [latest release](link:releases).
2. Replace the `Hyperspace.*.so` file in your FTL folder with the one for the same FTL
   version, normally `Hyperspace.1.6.13.amd64.so` on a Deck copy from Steam. Keep only
   one.

<Aside type="tip" title="Not sure which version you are on">
Launch FTL and read the version from the **bottom right** of the main menu.
</Aside>

Leave the `LD_PRELOAD` line in your launch script alone, unless the filename changed.

<FtlmanUpdate />

<Aside type="caution" title="Running Multiverse?">
Re-patch Multiverse with your mod manager after updating. Saves survive this.
</Aside>
