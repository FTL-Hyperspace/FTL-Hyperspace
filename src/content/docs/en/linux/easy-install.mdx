ftlman is the mod manager built for Hyperspace. It downloads whichever Hyperspace
release you pick, applies it to your copy of FTL, and keeps your mods in one list you
can reorder and re-apply whenever you like. Nothing needs downgrading on Linux.

<Steps>

1. ### Steam settings

   Skip this if you did not buy FTL on Steam.

   <SteamSettings />

   <Saves />

2. ### Set up ftlman

   <Ftlman />

3. ### Install Hyperspace

   <FtlmanHyperspace />

4. ### Add your mods

   <FtlmanMods />

   <Aside type="caution" title="Installing Multiverse?">
   Leave your mods out for now and follow the Multiverse section below instead.
   </Aside>

5. ### Check it worked

   <Verify />

</Steps>

**Hyperspace is now installed and ready to play.** Everything below is optional.

<Multiverse ftlmanOnly />

## Updating

Open ftlman, pick the newer release from the **Hyperspace** dropdown and press
**Apply**. Your saves are not affected.
