## What it does

FTL's own modding only lets you rearrange what the game already has. Hyperspace patches
the game itself and removes that limit.

With it, mods can add new species with their own stats and abilities, an unlimited ship
roster, custom systems and subsystems, augments that combine into new ones, custom
drones, stores and events, and Lua scripting for behaviour regular patching cannot do.

That is why [FTL: Multiverse](link:multiverseForum) and nearly every other large mod
require it.

Install it on its own and you get seeded runs to share or race, a numerical hull
indicator, Discord Rich Presence, and a long list of fixes for bugs in the base game.

It runs natively on Windows, macOS and Linux, the Steam Deck included.

## For mod makers

Most of what Hyperspace exposes is documented in `data/hyperspace.xml` inside
`Hyperspace.ftl`. Use a `hyperspace.xml.append` and add the tags your mod needs.

Ship your mod expecting the player to download Hyperspace separately and patch it
first. For the best compatibility with other mods, do not redistribute Hyperspace's
resource files or binaries, just point people at the download in your forum post.

The Lua API for 1.2.0 and above lives in [the wiki](link:wiki), along with the rest of
the developer documentation. Improvements are welcome: open an issue on GitHub with a
markdown snippet or a link to a clone of the wiki repository.
