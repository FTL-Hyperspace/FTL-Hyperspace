### macOS blocked the app

Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll to the bottom, and click
**Open Anyway**. On older macOS versions, right-click the app and choose **Open**.

### The game will not start after a manual install

You almost certainly skipped the signing step. Run it again:

<Codesign />

<Aside type="caution" title="Launch the app directly">
Starting FTL through Steam bypasses the patched app bundle. Open `FTL.app` yourself.
</Aside>

### Still stuck on a Mac

macOS problems have their own channel on the Multiverse Discord:
[#mac-support](link:discordMacSupport). Say which macOS version you are on, whether
you used the installer or installed by hand, and where you bought FTL.
