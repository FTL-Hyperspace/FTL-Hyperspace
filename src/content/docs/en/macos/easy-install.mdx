The macOS installer finds your copy of FTL, installs Hyperspace and sets up ftlman
for you.

<Video src={tutorial} caption="Running the installer, then adding Multiverse on top. Written steps below." />

<KeepVanilla />

<Steps>

1. ### Steam settings

   Skip this if you did not buy FTL on Steam.

   <SteamSettings />

   <MacosSaves />

   <BackupSaves />

2. ### Download the installer

   From the [latest release](link:releases), download
   `FTL.Hyperspace.x.xx.x-Installer-MacOS-arm64.dmg` for Apple Silicon, or
   `...-Installer-MacOS-x86_64.dmg` for an Intel Mac.

3. ### Run it

   Open the `.dmg` and launch the installer.

   <Aside type="tip" title='"Apple could not verify this app"'>
   Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, find the installer near the bottom,
   and click **Open Anyway**.
   </Aside>

4. ### Follow the prompts

   The installer detects Steam, GOG and custom locations, and supports FTL 1.6.12 and
   1.6.13. At the end, use the button at the bottom to open the mods folder.

   To add mods later, go to `~/Games/FTLHyperspace`. Put them in `mods` and run
   `ftlman` to apply them.

</Steps>

**Hyperspace is now installed and ready to play.** Everything below is optional.

## Adding Multiverse

<Multiverse ftlmanOnly />

<Aside type="note" title="Updating later">
The installer can update Hyperspace on its own. If you run Multiverse, run ftlman
again afterwards to re-patch it. Your saves are not affected.
</Aside>

## Uninstalling

Open the `.dmg` again and choose **Uninstall** at the bottom, then follow the
instructions.

<Screenshot src={uninstallButton} alt="The installer window with the Uninstall button at the bottom" label="Show me the button" />

## Requirements

macOS 14 or later, and FTL 1.6.12 or 1.6.13. Administrator rights are needed during
installation.
