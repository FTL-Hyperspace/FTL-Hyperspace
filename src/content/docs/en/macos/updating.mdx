<Tabs syncKey="macos-method">
<TabItem label="Installer">

<Steps>

1. Download the newest `.dmg` from the [latest release](link:releases) and run the
   installer again. It updates Hyperspace in place.

2. Open the mods folder (`~/Games/FTLHyperspace`) with the button at the end of the
   installer, and run `ftlman` again to re-patch your mods.

</Steps>

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Manual">

<Steps>

1. Download `FTL.Hyperspace.x.xx.x-MacOS.zip` from the [latest release](link:releases).

2. Replace the dylib in `FTL.app/Contents/MacOS` with the one for your FTL version:
   `Hyperspace.1.6.13.amd64.dylib` or `Hyperspace.1.6.12.amd64.dylib`.

   <VersionCheck />

3. Copy `Hyperspace.ftl` from the ZIP into your mod manager's `mods` folder, replacing
   the old one.

4. If the game fails to start, sign the app again:

   <Codesign />

5. Run your mod manager again and patch your mods.

</Steps>

</TabItem>
</Tabs>
